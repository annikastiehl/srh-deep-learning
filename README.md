## Working in Google Colab

1. Click **Open in Colab** next to the lab.
2. **File → Save a copy in Drive** — otherwise your changes are lost when the session ends.
3. **Runtime → Change runtime type → T4 GPU.**
4. The free tier limits GPU time per day: debug on small data or on CPU first, then switch to the GPU.

## Try it in your browser (no GPU, no install)

**Day 1**
- [TensorFlow Playground](https://playground.tensorflow.org) — watch a small network learn (XOR, hidden layers, learning rate)
- [Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com) — train an image or audio classifier with your webcam / microphone
- [3Blue1Brown: Neural networks](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks) — visual intuition for networks, gradient descent and backpropagation
- [Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu) — probability and regression, interactive

**Day 2**
- [A Visual and Interactive Guide to the Basics of Neural Networks](https://jalammar.github.io/visual-interactive-guide-basics-neural-networks/#train_your_dragon) (Jay Alammar) — "train your dragon": fit one neuron by hand
- [Backprop Explainer](https://xnought.github.io/backprop-explainer/) — watch forward and backward passes while a small network learns
- [3Blue1Brown: What is backpropagation really doing?](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/backpropagation) — backpropagation visually, almost no formulas

**Day 3**
- [Why Momentum Really Works](https://distill.pub/2017/momentum/) (Distill) — play with step size and momentum, watch the path to the minimum
- [TensorFlow Playground](https://playground.tensorflow.org) — learning rate too small / too large; overfitting with little data and L1 / L2 regularisation

**Day 4**
- [Image kernels explained visually](https://setosa.io/ev/image-kernels/) — blur, sharpen, Sobel, outline: try filters on an image
- [CNN Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/cnn-explainer/) — click through a small trained CNN
- [3D CNN visualisation](https://adamharley.com/nn_vis/cnn/3d.html) — draw a digit and watch every layer
- [Convolution arithmetic](https://github.com/vdumoulin/conv_arithmetic) — animations of padding, stride and dilation

**Day 5**
- [Understanding LSTM Networks](https://colah.github.io/posts/2015-08-Understanding-LSTMs/) (Chris Olah) — RNN, LSTM and GRU step by step, the classic pictures
- [The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks](https://karpathy.github.io/2015/05/21/rnn-effectiveness/) (Andrej Karpathy) — an RNN writes Shakespeare and Linux code letter by letter; single memory units that track quotes and line length
- [VizLearn: interactive 3D RNN visualizer](https://vizlearn.in/natural_language_processing/rnn.html) (Ashish Jangra) — an unrolled RNN in 3D: many-to-one / many-to-many, sequence length, stacked layers
- [Memorization in RNNs](https://distill.pub/2019/memorization-in-rnns/) (Distill) — interactive: which earlier letters do GRU and LSTM use?
- [CS230 cheat sheet: Recurrent neural networks](https://stanford.edu/~shervine/teaching/cs-230/cheatsheet-recurrent-neural-networks/) (Afshine & Shervine Amidi, Stanford) — RNNs, BPTT, gates, LSTM/GRU on one page

**Day 6**
- [The Illustrated Transformer](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/) (Jay Alammar) — attention, query / key / value, multi-head and positions in pictures
- [3Blue1Brown: Transformers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wjZofJX0v4M) and [Attention in transformers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMlx5fFNoYc) — the videos from the lecture, in full
- [Embedding Projector](https://projector.tensorflow.org) — words as vectors in 3D: search a word and look at its neighbours
- [Transformer Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/transformer-explainer/) — type a sentence and watch GPT-2 predict the next word
- [LLM Visualization](https://bbycroft.net/llm) (Brendan Bycroft) — a whole GPT in 3D, every number visible
- [AI by Hand – Excel workbooks](https://github.com/ImagineAILab/ai-by-hand-excel) (Tom Yeh, CU Boulder) — compute self-attention, multi-head attention and a full transformer yourself, cell by cell; also MLP, backprop, RNN, LSTM
- [Spreadsheets are all you need](https://spreadsheets-are-all-you-need.ai/) (Ishan Anand) — all of GPT-2 small in one Excel file (Excel 365), plus a browser version: follow every step from token to next-word prediction
- [Stanford CME 295 cheat sheet: Transformers & LLMs](https://github.com/afshinea/stanford-cme-295-transformers-large-language-models/blob/main/en/cheatsheet-transformers-large-language-models.pdf) (Afshine & Shervine Amidi) — everything on one page

**Later in the course**
- [GAN Lab](https://poloclub.github.io/ganlab/) — train a GAN on 2D data and watch it evolve (day 7)
- [Diffusion Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/diffusion-explainer/) — Stable Diffusion step by step (day 7)
- [Embedding Atlas](https://apple.github.io/embedding-atlas/) — explore clusters and outliers in embedding spaces (day 7)
- [PAIR AI Explorables](https://pair.withgoogle.com/explorables/), [Neuronpedia](https://www.neuronpedia.org/) — generalisation, interpretability, model behaviour (day 8)
- [Distill](https://distill.pub/) — visual essays on ML and interpretability

**Beyond this course: LLMs**
- [Transformer concepts](https://www.abhik.ai/concepts/transformers) (Abhik Sarkar) — RoPE, KV cache, FlashAttention, MQA/GQA
- [A Visual Guide to Attention Variants in Modern LLMs](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/visual-attention-variants) (Sebastian Raschka) — MHA, MQA, GQA, MLA
- [A Visual Guide to Mixture of Experts](https://www.maartengrootendorst.com/blog/moe/) (Maarten Grootendorst)
- [LLM Inference Handbook](https://handbook.modular.com/) (Modular) — prefill, decode, KV cache, batching, quantisation
